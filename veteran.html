For veterans // What’s next?

Find opponents. Roll dice. Keep EAV playing.

More games.
Stronger scene.

Our planned game nights will include structured Recruit tables, but the night is not only for newcomers. Meet at Joystick, find an opponent, and head to Dice City for a pickup game, or arrange a matchup ahead of time in the East Atlanta Comics / Dice City Discord.

We want to give local players a reliable rally point, expand the tabletop scene in EAV, and put more games on more tables. Every gathering supports Dice City, Joystick, and the independent nerd spaces that give this neighborhood somewhere to connect.

  1. Launching Fall 2026

    Happy hour and pickup games

    Bring an army and form a same-night matchup before the walk to Dice City. Use Discord to line up an opponent or agree on a system and game size in advance.

    Find a matchup on Discord →
  2. Growing next

    League play

    Regular pairings will make it easier to find opponents, try new armies, and keep a campaign moving. Our first friendly bracket will reward participation, games played, and varied achievements instead of tracking win-loss standings.

  3. Future objective

    Tournaments and prizes

    As the player base and table capacity grow, we plan to run competitive events with prizes alongside the casual games, campaigns, and pickup nights that keep the wider community active.

Bring the game
you want to play.

Our first teaching events focus on popular, approachable games, but this community loves tabletop games and miniature wargames of every size, age, and level of obscurity.

Bring what you want to play, teach, or share. Talk to a Commander at Happy Hour or suggest a game in the Dice City Discord. We’ll always try to help interested players find one another and get another game onto the table.
