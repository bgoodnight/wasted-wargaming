The commander path

Pass the hobby forward.

Commanders help welcome new players, teach games, set up tables, and make sure nobody has to figure out the hobby alone.

We’re looking for people with patience, passion, and a love of tabletop games. Together, we can break down barriers, challenge tired stereotypes about the hobby, and welcome new players with open arms.

You do not need encyclopedic rules knowledge or a tournament record. A great Commander can explain a choice without taking over, celebrate curiosity, recover gracefully when a rule is unclear, and make the person across the table feel like they belong.

01

Welcome

Meet recruits at Happy Hour, make introductions, and lower the social pressure of arriving alone.

02

Prepare

Help arrange welcoming tables, reference sheets, terrain, miniatures, and the supplies a first game needs.

03

Teach

Guide the rules at the players’ pace, invite questions, and keep the focus on a good shared experience.

04

Connect

Help new players find their next game and become familiar faces in the EAV tabletop community.

What matters here

Patience over prestige.

Rules expertise helps. The ability to create a relaxed, judgment-free table matters more.

  • Teach the player, not just the rules.Pause, listen, and adjust to what your table needs.
  • Keep the gate open.No one should be judged for their knowledge, army, painting, identity, or pace.
  • Make room for fun.A memorable first game is more important than flawless play.
